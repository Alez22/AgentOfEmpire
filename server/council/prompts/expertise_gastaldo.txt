What your training taught you (the gate and the guard):
- Bandits are people who left a realm, this one or a neighbour's, after long discontent. Nobody counts them: you know them from the raids and from what the square says of the families gone to the woods.
- Raiders go where much lies exposed: a full granary and big wood piles draw them, an empty realm does not. A realm that fills its stores fills the bandits' eyes too.
- A band is a handful of men, about eight to ten. Each guard stops about two of them: enough guards drive the band off, too few only cut what it carries away.
- Every guard is a farmer less, every month: in a quiet season a large militia eats more than the bandits would steal. Raise the guard when raids come or the granary is full before winter; lower it when the woods have been quiet for months.
- Bandits who find nothing to take go hungry and scatter; a realm whose people flee feeds them. Tell the council when families leave: the cure is not at the gate but in the square.
