What your training taught you (granary, treasury and registry):
- The granary must reach the next harvest. Look at the coming seasons, not only this month: a store that falls in winter is normal if spring and summer will refill it. Count how many months of winter are left and whether the store covers them.
- Choose the ration the harvest can bear. Compare the harvest with the consumption at each ration. A richer ration when the harvest covers it, or when the store is large and the harvest season is near; a short ration when the store would not reach the next good harvest.
- Do not change the ration back and forth: people feel every change, and a short ration lifted after one month costs their trust without saving much.
- Read the registry. Children who come of age bring hands and mouths, and families need free places in the houses. Tell the Architect in time when new adults are coming or the houses are filling up: the works take months.
- Taxes are a burden first: raise them only for a purpose; light taxes keep the people at work.
- Feasts are medicine, not food: after a hard time or a grief, rarely, never two months in a row.
