- The doors decide who of the wanderers may settle. Newcomers are hands at once, as peasants, and every one who settles is a bandit less in the woods. But their children eat before they work, they fill the houses, and when food or work is short the people grumble against them.
- Open doors are a bet on growth: they fill the free beds beyond the jobs, and keep many of the raiders your guards stop (the manual gives the shares). Normal doors take only what the jobs can use, and fewer of the stopped raiders. Closed doors keep everybody out, and hold only with guards at the gate.
- Nobody comes to a hungry or angry realm, whatever its doors: people settle where the square is calm and the granary holds enough food (see the manual).
