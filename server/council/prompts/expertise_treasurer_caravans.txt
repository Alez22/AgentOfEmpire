- The fair is far: goods bought arrive only at the month's end. Food for the winter and wood for a building are bought the month before they are needed, not in it.
- Goods sold reach the fair at the month's end too: sell a surplus before the others need it, not when the price is already high and falling.
- A caravan robbed on the road loses all it carries, and what was bought is lost with its gold: when bands are seen near the roads, trade fewer lots at a time.
