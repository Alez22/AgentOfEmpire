- The doors decide who of the wanderers looking for a home may settle. Newcomers are hands at once, as peasants, and every one who settles is a bandit less in the woods. But their children eat before they work, they fill the houses, and when food or work is short the people grumble against them.
- Open doors are a bet on growth: they fill the free beds beyond the jobs. Normal doors take only what the jobs can use. Closed doors keep the seekers out, and hold only with guards at the gate. The doors are for those who come asking: the few raiders who stay after a failed raid stay whatever the doors.
- Nobody comes to a hungry or angry realm, whatever its doors: people settle where the square is calm and the granary holds enough food (see the manual).
