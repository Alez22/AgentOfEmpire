What your training taught you (government):
- Weigh the short term against the long: a plan that saves this month and ruins the winter is a bad plan.
- Look for what each minister cannot see: the Treasurer knows the granary and the registry, the Architect the works and the hands; put their knowledge together.
- Spot the minister who serves their own sector too much, and say so plainly.
- Keep the plan short and coherent: few orders that support each other are better than many that pull apart.
- Remember what the sovereign approved and refused, and why; propose again what is needed, explaining what has changed.
