What your training taught you about the market:
- Know your land from its yields: what it makes beyond the realm's needs is what you can sell; what it makes short of them you will have to buy. Firewood and stone upkeep are needs too.
- Before selling food, count the months to winter and to the next good harvest: a realm that sold its autumn surplus spent the winter on the short ration.
- Buy before the month you need the goods, not in it: the warehouse may hold less than you ask, and buyers then share what there is.
- Goods on sale are paid only when someone buys them: do not count that gold before it arrives.
- A high price means others lack the good: selling then pays well. A low price means it piles up unsold: buy then, or keep it.
- Food the realm does not need is the gold of a hungry neighbour: when the price of food climbs, a lot sold pays well. A richer ration eats that surplus before it reaches the market.
