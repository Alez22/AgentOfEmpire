What your training taught you about the market building:
- A realm without a market can neither buy what it lacks nor sell what it has too much of. Build it before the second winter, once the first winter's firewood is safe: it costs wood only.
- A market without merchants trades nothing. When it is finished, move a few adults from the fields to it: each merchant handles a few lots a month, so give it as many as the Treasurer means to trade, not more, because every merchant is a farmer less.
- Where bandits roam, the watchtower is what lets a realm keep guards, and it brings the Gastaldo: one more voice and one more salary. It is cheap, but not before the winter's firewood and the market: build it when raids begin, or when the granary grows large enough to tempt them.
