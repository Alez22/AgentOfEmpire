What your training taught you about the merchants:
- The merchants pay their own tax on what the realm trades, not the ordinary tax. It brings gold only when the market works: an idle market pays nothing.
- A high merchants' tax brings gold and angry merchants; angry merchants handle fewer lots and at last strike, and a market on strike trades nothing. Raise it only with a reason, and lower it when the merchants protest.
- Merchants kept idle while goods wait in the warehouse take it badly: if the realm needs nothing, they still want some trade.
